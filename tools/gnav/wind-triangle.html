061 · GENERAL NAVIGATION

Wind triangle

Solve the triangle of velocities: heading and groundspeed for a desired track, or the wind from what you actually flew. Directions in °True, speeds in knots.

Inputs

Optional

Variation west, magnetic best · variation east, magnetic least.

  • Air vector
    Heading / TAS
  • Wind vector
    Wind direction / speed
  • Ground vector
    Track / GS

Working

    Exam tips

    • Drift is from heading to track. Wind from the left gives right drift, so you head left of the track (into the wind).
    • Max drift ≈ WS × 60 / TAS. Multiply it by the sine of the wind angle (30° → ½, 45° → 0.7, 60° → 0.9).
    • Wind direction is where it blows from (TAF/METAR in °T, ATIS/tower in °M). The wind vector points downwind.
    • The wind changes GS by up to ±WS. A pure crosswind still costs you a little groundspeed (TAS × cos WCA).
