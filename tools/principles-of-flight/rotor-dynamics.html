Model: rigid blades with flap and lag degrees of freedom, linear twist, a lift curve that stalls at 15° (allowing for dynamic stall), momentum inflow with a fore-aft gradient, and a fuselage attitude that follows a simple steady-flight law (the free-stream inflow assumes the disc sits at its steady-flight angle). Values are indicative and chosen to make each effect visible; they are not data for a particular helicopter. Flapping and lead-lag are drawn exaggerated by the slider (lead-lag twice as much).
