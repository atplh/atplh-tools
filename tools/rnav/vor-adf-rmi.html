VOR, ADF and RMI

Move the helicopter around a VOR/DME and an NDB and watch what the RMI, the fixed-card ADF and the VOR indicator show. Map north is magnetic north (variation 0).

Drag the helicopter, the VOR or the NDB. Click anywhere to move the helicopter there. ← → turn, space flies.

Readouts

Card = heading · needles = QDM

Needle = relative bearing

Practice

Press “New question” for a random position. Read the instruments, not the map.

Key relationships

  • QDM = MH + RB (ADF). The RMI does this sum for you: the needle head reads QDM, the tail reads QDR.
  • VOR radial = QDR, magnetic bearing from the station. QDM = QDR ± 180.
  • The VOR indicator does not depend on heading. It only shows where you are relative to the selected course. Turning the helicopter changes nothing on it.
  • With the OBS set to your QDM the needle centres with TO. Set to the radial (QDR) it centres with FROM.
  • Fly the heading that matches the OBS and the needle points towards the course (no reverse sensing).
